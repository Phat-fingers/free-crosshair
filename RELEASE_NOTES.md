## Download

Click **FreeCrosshair.exe** below, then double-click it. Nothing to install.

The first time, Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**. That happens because the app isn't code-signed (signing costs money). Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's in it

- Crosshair styles: cross, T-shape, X, dot, circle, circle + cross, or your own PNG image
- Color, length, thickness, gap, center dot, outline, opacity, position nudge
- Profiles, switched with a hotkey while gaming
- Share codes to swap crosshairs with friends
- Hotkeys: **F8** show/hide, **F9** next profile, **F10** settings (changeable)
- Runs from the system tray; optional start with Windows
